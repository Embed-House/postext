# The class money box

## A fund for everyone

In September, Year 4 decided to raise money for a trip to the :chip[railway museum.]{style="clave"} Each pupil puts in what they can, and the teacher writes it all down in a notebook. That way the group learns how a :chip[small economy]{style="clave"} works.

- :chip[Income.]{style="clave"} It comes from a cake sale and a stall of second-hand books.
- :chip[Spending.]{style="clave"} It leaves the box to pay for the coach, the tickets and lunch.

:::frame{slot="dinero" label="Where the money comes from"}
The money in the box is earned by work: baking, painting posters and running the book stall.

Some families pay in **cash** and others with a **phone**. The teacher keeps every **receipt** so the accounts add up.
:::

::resource{id="foto-dinero" slot="dinero-fotos"}

:::frame{slot="gasto" label="What it is spent on"}
Before spending anything, the class makes a list and puts it in order:

- **Needed:** the coach and the tickets; without them there is no trip.
- **Useful:** a hot lunch at midday.
- **A treat:** a souvenir from the museum shop, if anything is left.
:::

::resource{id="foto-gasto" slot="gasto-fotos"}

:::frame{slot="ahorro" label="Keeping some for later"}
If there is money left at the end, nobody shares it out: it is **saved** for next year. A simple **budget**, with what comes in and what goes out each month, avoids surprises and shows how far the class is from its goal.
:::
