# Varenia on the map

Varenia is a country in the south of the continent, with Ostelia to the north and Brunavia to the east. The Lira Sea washes its west coast and the Misty Ocean its south coast, which the Gulf of Sora cuts into. It covers about 40,000 km² and has nearly four million people.

The capital, Almaren, stands on the River Merel a few kilometres from Lake Ormo. Almost half the people live on the coast, in port towns such as Calvera, Tessa and Port Sora. This map shows where Varenia is; the next two pages show its relief and its rivers.

# The relief and rivers of Varenia

The Albar Range crosses the west of the country from south-west to north-east. It is the highest range in Varenia: Albar Peak reaches 2,565 m. In the east rise the Grey Hills, lower and more rounded. Between the two lies the Ormo Plateau, about 600 m above the sea.

The rivers rise in the mountains and run down to the sea. The Sela flows west and reaches the sea at Calvera. The Duna, short and fast, runs south. The Merel, the longest, passes through Lake Ormo and flows into the Gulf of Sora. The line A–B marks the section on Plate 3.

# A section through the Albar Range

A section shows the shape of the land as if it were cut along a line. This one follows the line A–B on the relief map: it starts in the hills of the north, drops into the Sela valley, climbs the Albar Range to more than 2,100 m and ends on the plain of the south coast.

The line A–B is 126 km long. To make the mountains easy to see, the vertical scale is stretched about fourteen times, so the slopes in the drawing look much steeper than they are.

:::frame{slot="key" title="Words to know"}
**Range:** a line of mountains whose peaks stand in a row.

**Plateau:** a high, flat stretch of land, more than 400 m above the sea.

**Valley:** low land between hills or mountains, often with a river along it.

**Plain:** low, nearly flat land, like the land along the south coast.
:::
