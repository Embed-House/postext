## Warm up {kicker="Before you start" letras="a b c" pic="grip"}

Sit up straight with both feet on the floor. Hold your pencil between your thumb and first finger, resting on your middle finger, and do not squeeze. Trace the dots from left to right without lifting your pencil.


:::callout{type="trazos" title="Waves on the sea" pattern="waves"}
:::

:::callout{type="trazos" title="Loops like a spring" pattern="loops"}
:::

## Round letters {kicker="Lesson 1" letras="c a d" pic="duck"}

The letters **c, a, d, g, o** and **q** all start the same way: go up to the top of the middle line and round back like a **c**. Each one ends with a little flick that joins it to the next letter.

:::callout{type="palabra" title="Trace and write"}
c a d g o
:::

:::callout{type="palabra" title="Trace and write"}
dog
:::

Which letter is missing? :chip[ ]{style="hueco"}at · d:chip[ ]{style="hueco"}g · c:chip[ ]{style="hueco"}t · b:chip[ ]{style="hueco"}d

:::callout{type="recuerda" title="Remember"}
Start every round letter at the top, at **one o'clock**, and go round anticlockwise.
:::

## Sh, ch and th {kicker="Lesson 2" letras="sh ch th" pic="fish"}

Two letters can make one sound. **sh** is the sound in fish and shop, **ch** is the sound in chip and lunch, and **th** is the sound in thumb and this. Join the two letters without lifting your pencil.

:::callout{type="palabra" title="Trace and write"}
fish
:::

:::callout{type="palabra" title="Trace and write"}
chip
:::

sh, ch or th? :chip[ ]{style="hueco"}op · lun:chip[ ]{style="hueco"} · ba:chip[ ]{style="hueco"} · :chip[ ]{style="hueco"}in

:::callout{type="recuerda" title="Remember"}
The **h** is a tall letter: its loop reaches the top line.
:::

## Ck and ng {kicker="Lesson 3" letras="ck ng" pic="kite"}

After a short vowel, the sound **k** at the end of a word is spelt **ck**: duck, sock, back. The sound in ring and long is spelt **ng**, and the g hangs below the line.

:::callout{type="palabra" title="Trace and write"}
duck
:::

:::callout{type="palabra" title="Trace and write"}
ring
:::

Finish the word: so:chip[ ]{style="hueco"} · si:chip[ ]{style="hueco"} · ba:chip[ ]{style="hueco"} · lo:chip[ ]{style="hueco"}

:::callout{type="recuerda" title="Remember"}
**ck** never starts a word. Cat and kit start with c and k.
:::

## Magic e {kicker="Lesson 4" letras="a–e" pic="cake"}

An **e** at the end of a word is silent, but it changes the vowel before it: it says its name. **kit** becomes **kite**, **cap** becomes **cape** and **hop** becomes **hope**.

:::callout{type="palabra" title="Trace and write"}
kite
:::

:::callout{type="palabra" title="Trace and write"}
cake
:::

Add the magic e and read: tap, :chip[tape]{style="puntos"} · pin, :chip[pine]{style="puntos"} · cub, :chip[cube]{style="puntos"}

:::callout{type="recuerda" title="Remember"}
The magic e joins to the letter before it like any other letter.
:::

## Ee, ai and oa {kicker="Lesson 5" letras="ee oa" pic="bee"}

Two vowels can work together. **ee** says its name in bee and tree, **ai** in rain and snail, and **oa** in boat and coat. Join the two vowels with a short line along the middle.

:::callout{type="palabra" title="Trace and write"}
bee
:::

:::callout{type="palabra" title="Trace and write"}
boat
:::

ee, ai or oa? tr:chip[ ]{style="hueco"} · r:chip[ ]{style="hueco"}n · g:chip[ ]{style="hueco"}t · sh:chip[ ]{style="hueco"}p

:::callout{type="recuerda" title="Remember"}
The join from **o** starts at the top, along the middle line.
:::

## Tricky words {kicker="Lesson 6" letras="th" pic="owl"}

Some words do not sound the way they are spelt. You learn them by heart: **the**, **said**, **was**, **they**, **you**. Look at the word, say it, cover it and write it.

:::callout{type="palabra" title="Trace and write"}
said
:::

:::callout{type="palabra" title="Trace and write"}
they
:::

Copy each word once: the · was · you · said

:::callout{type="recuerda" title="Remember"}
Look, say, cover, write and check: five steps for every tricky word.
:::

## Capital letters {kicker="Lesson 7" letras="A B" pic="house"}

Names and the first word of a sentence start with a **capital letter**. Capital letters do not join to the letter after them, so lift your pencil after each one.

:::callout{type="palabra" title="Trace and write"}
Anna
:::

:::callout{type="palabra" title="Trace and write"}
Leeds
:::

Write each name with a capital: tom · leeds · may · london

:::callout{type="recuerda" title="Remember"}
**I** is always a capital when you write about yourself.
:::

## Copy a sentence {kicker="Lesson 8" letras="Aa" pic="butterfly"}

A sentence starts with a capital letter and ends with a full stop. Leave a space the size of a small o between words.

:::callout{type="frase" title="Copy the sentence"}
My cat sleeps in the sun.
:::

:::callout{type="frase" title="Copy the sentence"}
The moon is up at night.
:::

:::callout{type="frase" title="Copy the sentence"}
I like to write.
:::
