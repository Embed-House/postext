# Water on the move {nombre="Name" fecha="Date"}

The water on Earth is always moving. The Sun warms the sea, the water rises into the sky and comes back down as rain or snow. This round trip is called the water cycle.

:::exercise
Circle the correct answer.

1. Water that falls from the clouds is called
   - [ ] steam
   - [x] rain
   - [ ] fog
2. When the Sun warms the sea, the water
   - [x] evaporates
   - [ ] freezes
   - [ ] sinks
:::

:::exercise{type="truefalse"}
Tick true or false.

- [x] Clouds are made of tiny drops of water.
- [ ] Ice melts when it gets colder.
- [x] Rivers carry water back to the sea.
- [ ] Rain is salty because it comes from the sea.
:::

:::exercise{bank}
Fill in the gaps with words from the box.

::bank[ice | wind]

The Sun warms the [[sea]]. The water turns into [[vapour]] and rises. High in the sky it cools down and forms [[clouds]]. Then it falls as [[rain]] or snow.
:::

:::exercise
Join each word to what it means.

- Evaporation -> Water turns into vapour
- Condensation -> Vapour turns into drops
- Precipitation -> Water falls from the clouds
- Infiltration -> Water soaks into the ground
:::
