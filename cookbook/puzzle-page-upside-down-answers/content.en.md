# Reef Puzzles {kicker="Puzzle page" sub="Solve them all, then turn the magazine round to check your answers."}

:::exercise{type=crossword}
**Sea-creature crossword**

Read the clues and fill in the grid.

- OCTOPUS: Eight arms and three hearts
- SHARK: A fish whose teeth grow back all its life
- CRAB: Walks sideways along the sand
- WHALE: The biggest animal that has ever lived
- CORAL: A reef is built by millions of these tiny animals
- SEAL: Barks on the rocks and swims like a torpedo
:::

:::exercise{type=wordsearch size=9 directions="across down diagonal backwards"}
**Rock-pool word search**

Find the eight words. They run across, down, diagonally and even backwards.

- SHELL
- WAVE
- SAND
- KELP
- REEF
- TIDE
- PEARL
- SQUID
:::

:::exercise
**Riddle of the deep**

I change colour to hide, I squirt ink when I am scared, and I can squeeze through a hole the size of a coin. Who am I?

- [ ] A starfish
- [x] An octopus
- [ ] A jellyfish
:::

:::exercise{type=order}
**From egg to ocean**

Put the story in order. Write 1 to 5 in the boxes.

::resource{id="hatchling"}

1. A mother turtle lays her eggs in the warm sand.
2. Two months later, the hatchlings dig their way out.
3. They race across the beach to the sea at night.
4. They grow up far away in the open ocean.
5. Years later, they come back to the same beach.
:::

:::exercise{type=scramble}
**Mixed-up fact**

Put the words in order to find a true fact, then write it on the line.

- Seahorse / dads / carry / the / babies / in / a / pouch
:::

:::exercise
**Baby names**

Join each sea animal to the name of its baby.

- Whale -> calf
- Seal -> pup
- Turtle -> hatchling
- Seahorse -> fry
:::
