# Mm {kicker="Letter by Letter · Phonics book 1" unit="Unit 12" name="the sound m"}

## Listen and say {style="oye"}

:::paragraphs{style="consigna"}
Say each sound, then blend the sounds into a word. In the second row, the m comes last.
:::

:::paragraphs{style="fila"}
{m·a·p} {m·e·n} {m·o·p}

{S·a·m} {d·i·m} {g·u·m}
:::

## Read the words {style="lee"}

:::paragraphs{style="consigna"}
A word takes the colour of its vowel; the m is filled. The word below it rhymes with it.

## Read the sentences {style="lee"}

:::paragraphs{style="consigna"}
Read slowly. The first time a new word comes up, it is split into its sounds.
:::

:::paragraphs{style="lectura"}
Sam and {T·o·m} sat on a mat.

Sam and Tom got mud on it.

Tom got {M·u·m}.

Mum got a mop.
:::

:::callout{type="familia" title="For parents and carers"}
Read the words on page 37: your child says each sound, then blends them. Say *mmm* with closed lips, not *muh*. If your child mixes up *map* and *mop*, do not say the word: point to the middle sound and ask its colour. Ten minutes a day is enough; stop while your child still wants more.
:::
