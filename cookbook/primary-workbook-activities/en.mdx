## What you'll build

Unit 5 of a science activity book for Year 3, on A4: *Animals with backbones*. The opener sets the unit in a green band over a painting of a pond; after it come eleven activities, each with its number in a green disc and a pencil, speech or pair icon. A word bank in a dashed tinted box, sentences with T and F to tick, animals to join to their groups with a line, words to circle in a sentence and in a short text, frog stages to number, a wordsearch, animals to sort, an open question on lines and another on squared paper. A Spanish edition prints the same unit as its own schools set it, with V and F and *une con flechas*. Three paintings, generated with a diffusion model, illustrate it.

## Method

### 1 · Write the activities in the Markdown, style them once

<Excerpt region="answer" />

Each activity is an `:::exercise` fence. What the pupil reads and what counts as right are in the text: `[[hair]]` is a blank whose answer is *hair*, `- [x]` a true statement, `- Dolphin -> Mammal` a pair, `[[pelo | (plumas)]]` a choice whose right word is in brackets. The style holds only the look, so the eleven activities share one `actividad` and a second, `cuadricula`, that `extends` it to swap the lines for squared paper. The engine numbers them, shuffles the right column of the pairs and the order items with a seed, makes the blanks as wide as the longest answer so none gives itself away, and prints T and F in English and V and F in Spanish without a word in the config.

### 2 · Give the activities their icons

<Excerpt region="icons" />

An icon in `exerciseStyles[].icons` names a picture of the book, and `:::exercise{icon="oral pareja"}` puts the pictures after the number, in the order written, read aloud by their `alt`. These are flat signs drawn in code on a disc of the unit colour, so they stay sharp in the PDF and change with the palette. A workbook also prints a pencil beside activities to write; the speaking and pair icons mark the last one, which has no writing area (`area="none"`).

### 3 · Open the unit with a band and a painting

<Excerpt region="opener" />

The unit's level-1 heading draws the band, the number pill and the title from its attributes (`unidad="Unit 5" pic="charca"`), and the painting under the band at 176 × 75 mm. The pictures inside activities use a resource type with no caption and no number (`dibujo`), as a workbook prints them. The text reads easily for young readers: Atkinson Hyperlegible Next at 12 pt on 17 pt, no hyphenation, ragged right.

## Variations

### Print the answers for the teacher

The same Markdown makes the teacher's copy: every blank filled in, the true boxes ticked and the right words ringed in magenta.

```diff
   exerciseStyles: [actividad, cuadricula],
+  exercises: { edition: 'teacher' },
```

### Number the activities in squares

A rounded square in the unit colour reads as another publisher's house style.

```diff
-  number: { shape: 'disc', background: col('unit'), color: col('paper'),
+  number: { shape: 'rounded', background: col('unit'), color: col('paper'),
```

## Pitfalls

- The words to circle in a running text keep a little room on each side for the ring, so a word right before a comma or a full stop stands slightly apart from it.
