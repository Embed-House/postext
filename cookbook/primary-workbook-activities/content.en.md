# Animals with backbones {unidad="Unit 5" pic="charca"}

**Vertebrates** are animals with a skeleton inside their bodies and a backbone. There are five groups: mammals, birds, fish, amphibians and reptiles. A pond can be home to animals from almost all of them.

:::callout{type="recuerda" title="Remember"}
To find out which group an animal belongs to, look at three things: what covers its body (hair, feathers, scales or bare skin), how it breathes (with lungs or with gills) and how it is born (from its mother's body or from an egg).
:::

:::callout{type="sabias" title="Did you know?"}
The platypus is a mammal that lays eggs. It lives in the rivers of Australia and has a beak like a duck's. When its babies hatch, it feeds them milk, like every other mammal.
:::
